ROOMTONE · Spotify

Connect
Spotify

The visualizer works without Spotify. Connecting it adds the track name, colours from the album art and word-by-word lyrics. It takes about two minutes.

One rule from Spotify you need to know. ROOMTONE connects through a small “developer app” on Spotify’s website, identified by a Client ID. Since March 2026, Spotify only lets that app work while the account that created it has Premium, and only for up to five people its owner adds by name. The people signing in can be on a free account.

Which one are you?

Make your own Spotify app

Do this once. Sign in with your Premium account.

  1. Open the developer dashboard

    Sign in with your normal Spotify login and accept the developer terms if it asks.

    OPEN THE DASHBOARD ↗

  2. Create an app

    Click Create app. Any name and description will do, for example “ROOMTONE”. Leave Website empty.

  3. Paste this exact Redirect URI

    In the Redirect URIs box, paste this and click Add. It must match exactly: 127.0.0.1, not “localhost”, and no slash at the end.

    http://127.0.0.1:8888/callback

  4. Tick “Web API” and save

    Under Which API/SDKs are you planning to use?, tick Web API. Agree to the terms and click Save.

  5. Add everyone who will sign in

    Open your app → Settings → User Management. Add yourself with the name and email of your Spotify account, then each friend (up to five people in total). Use the email on their Spotify account, not just any email they have.

    Skip this and Spotify refuses the sign-in with a 403.

  6. Copy the Client ID into ROOMTONE

    On the app’s Settings page, copy the Client ID: 32 letters and numbers. You do not need the Client secret.

    App nameROOMTONE
    Client ID3f9c…e21a ← this one
    Client secretnot needed

    In ROOMTONE, open Settings → Spotify → Set up, paste it and press Save. Your browser opens. Click Agree, then go back to the app.

Sharing with a friend on Free? Add them in step 05, then send them your Client ID. It is not a password: on its own it cannot sign anyone in or see your account.

Use a friend’s Spotify app

You need one friend with Spotify Premium. You can stay on Free.

  1. Send your friend your Spotify email

    The email on your Spotify account (Spotify app → Settings → Account). Also tell them the name on the account.

  2. They set up an app and add youyour friend

    If they do not have one yet, they follow path A. Then they add you in Settings → User Management and send you their Client ID.

  3. Paste their Client ID into ROOMTONE

    In ROOMTONE, open Settings → Spotify → Set up. If an old Client ID is saved, click Use a different Client ID first. Paste theirs and press Save.

  4. Sign in with your own account

    Your browser opens on Spotify. Sign in as you, not your friend, and click Agree. Then go back to ROOMTONE.

Their app holds up to five people, them included. If Premium on their account lapses, everyone on it loses the connection until it is renewed. The visualizer itself keeps working either way.

If it doesn’t connect

ROOMTONE shows Spotify’s reason under the Connect button. Find it below.

“the account that created the app does not have Premium”

The Client ID belongs to an account without Premium, and Spotify has switched that app off. Either the owner gets Premium (it can take a few hours to start working again), or you switch to a Client ID from someone who has it: path B.

“Spotify refused your account for this Client ID” / 403

The account you signed in with is not on the app’s User Management list. The app owner adds your Spotify account’s email there. If they did, check that you signed in with that same account in the browser. Spotify may have kept you logged in as someone else; log out at spotify.com/logout and connect again.

“INVALID_CLIENT: Invalid redirect URI” in the browser

The Redirect URI on the app does not match exactly. It must be http://127.0.0.1:8888/callback: http, 127.0.0.1 (not localhost), port 8888, no trailing slash. Edit it in the app’s Settings and press Save at the bottom.

“INVALID_CLIENT: Invalid client” or “That does not contain a Client ID”

The Client ID was copied wrong, or the app was deleted. Copy it again from the app’s Settings page. It is 32 characters. The Client secret is a different value and will not work.

“Another program may be using port 8888”

Something else on your PC is using port 8888 for a moment. Close other music tools or dev servers and try again. Restarting the PC also clears it.

“timed out waiting for Spotify to redirect back”

The browser page was left open for more than three minutes, or closed. Press Connect again and finish within three minutes.

Connected, but play / pause / skip don’t work

Spotify only allows playback control on your own Premium account. On Free you still get the track, the album colours and the lyrics. Control playback from the Spotify app instead.

Settings → Spotify opened a blank screen

That was a bug in ROOMTONE 0.3.0 and is fixed in the next release. Download the latest version.

What this gives ROOMTONE access to

Only what is playing and basic playback control. The sign-in token is kept in Windows Credential Manager on your PC and is never uploaded. ROOMTONE has no server. Privacy details.
